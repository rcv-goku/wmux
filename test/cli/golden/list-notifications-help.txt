Usage: cmux list-notifications [--json]

List queued notifications, newest first, one per line:
  <index>:<id>|<workspace_id>|<surface_id|none>|<read|unread>|<title>|<subtitle>|<body>|<created_at>|<tab_title>
Prints `No notifications` when the list is empty. --json prints an array of
{id, workspace_id, surface_id, is_read, title, subtitle, body, created_at, tab_title}.
