Usage: cmux rename-workspace [--workspace <id|ref|index>] [--] <title>

Rename a workspace (default: the caller's workspace) and print
"OK <workspace>". The remaining words form the title; use -- before a title
that starts with "--". Legacy alias of "cmux workspace rename";
rename-window is the tmux-compatible spelling and prints no legacy notice.

Options:
  --workspace <id|ref|index>   Workspace to rename
  --json                       Print the result as JSON
