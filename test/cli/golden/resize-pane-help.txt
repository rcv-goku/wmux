Usage: cmux resize-pane [--pane <id|ref|index>] [--workspace <id|ref|index>] [--window <id|ref|index>] [-L|-R|-U|-D] [--amount <n>]

Resize a pane (tmux-compatible): move its divider left, right, up or down.

Options:
  --pane <id|ref|index>         Pane to resize (default: the focused pane)
  --workspace <id|ref|index>    Workspace context (default: $CMUX_WORKSPACE_ID)
  --window <id|ref|index>       Window context for refs and indexes
  -L, -R, -U, -D                Direction (default: -R)
  --amount <n>                  Steps to move, at least 1 (default: 1)
  --json                        Print the full response

Example:
  cmux resize-pane -L --amount 5
