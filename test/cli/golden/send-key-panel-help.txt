Usage: cmux send-key-panel [--panel <id|ref|index>] [--force] <key>

The older, panel-addressed form of send-key: --panel is required, and key
names, the dialog guard and --force work as they do for send-key.

Options:
  --panel <id|ref|index>        Target surface (required)
  --workspace <id|ref|index>    Workspace context (default: $CMUX_WORKSPACE_ID)
  --window <id|ref|index>       Window context for refs and indexes
  --force                       Send even while an agent is asking a
                                question or for a permission
  --json                        Print the full response
