Usage: cmux clear-notifications [--workspace <id|ref|index>] [--surface <id|ref|index>] [--window <id|ref|index>]

Clear queued notifications. Without options it clears everything, except
inside a wmux terminal (CMUX_WORKSPACE_ID set), where it clears the caller's
workspace. --window alone clears that window's current workspace; --surface
(alias --panel) narrows to one surface and needs a workspace or window
context. Prints `OK`.
