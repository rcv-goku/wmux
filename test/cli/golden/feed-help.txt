Usage: cmux feed tui [--opentui|--legacy]
       cmux feed clear [--yes|-y]

Feed workstream history. `feed clear` permanently deletes the history file
%LOCALAPPDATA%\wmux\<channel>\workstream.jsonl (in WMUX_STATE_DIR when set)
after a [y/N] confirmation (skipped with --yes) and works without a running
app. `feed tui` is not available in this build of wmux yet.
