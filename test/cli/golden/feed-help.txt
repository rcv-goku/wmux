Usage: cmux feed tui [--opentui|--legacy]
       cmux feed clear [--yes|-y]

Feed workstream history. `feed clear` permanently deletes
%USERPROFILE%\.cmuxterm\workstream.jsonl after a [y/N] confirmation (skipped
with --yes) and works without a running app. `feed tui` is not available in
this build of wmux yet.
