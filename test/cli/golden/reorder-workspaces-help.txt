Usage: cmux reorder-workspaces --order <id|ref|index>,<id|ref|index>,... [--window <id|ref|index>] [--dry-run]

Atomically place the listed workspaces first, in order, within the pinned
and unpinned groups; unlisted workspaces keep their relative order. Prints
"OK".

Options:
  --order <list>               Comma-separated workspace handles
  --window <id|ref|index>      Window that resolves indexes
  --dry-run                    Print the resulting order, one handle per
                               line (or the planned moves as
                               "<workspace> from=<i> to=<j>"), without
                               applying it
  --json                       Print the result as JSON
