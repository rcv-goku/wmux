Usage: cmux wait-for [-S|--signal] <name> [--timeout <seconds>]

Wait for a named signal, or send one with -S. Works without the app.

A signal sent before the wait is kept until one waiter consumes it; each
signal wakes exactly one waiter. Without -S the command waits until the
signal arrives (exit 0) or the timeout passes (exit 1).

Options:
  -S, --signal                  Signal <name> instead of waiting
  --timeout <seconds>           Give up after this long (default: 30)

Signals are marker files in a private per-user directory (%TEMP%\wmux-wait-for
on Windows).

Examples:
  cmux wait-for build-done --timeout 120
  cmux wait-for -S build-done
