Usage: cmux move-surface --surface <id|ref|index> [--pane|--workspace|--window <id|ref|index>] [--index <n>]

Take a surface out of its pane and place it in another pane, workspace or
window, or at another position.

Options:
  --surface <id|ref|index>      Surface to move (required; alias --panel)
  --pane <id|ref|index>         Destination pane
  --workspace <id|ref|index>    Destination workspace
  --window <id|ref|index>       Destination window
  --index <n>                   Destination position (0-based)
  --focus <true|false>          Focus the moved surface (default: false)
  --json                        Print the full response
