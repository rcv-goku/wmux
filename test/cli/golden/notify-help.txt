Usage: cmux notify [--title <text>] [--subtitle <text>] [--body <text>] [--reply] [--desktop <true|false>] [--clear] [--workspace <id|ref|index>] [--surface <id|ref|index>] [--window <id|ref|index>] [--json] [--id-format refs|uuids|both]

Post a notification to the calling pane (or the given workspace/surface) and
print its id as `OK notification:<uuid>`. Prints a plain `OK` when the app
recorded nothing (muted workspace, policy, asynchronous hooks).

Options:
  --title <text>              Title (default: Notification)
  --subtitle <text>           Subtitle (default: empty)
  --body <text>               Body (default: empty)
  --reply                     Offer a Reply box on the desktop notification
  --desktop <true|false>      false records the entry without a desktop banner
  --clear                     Clear the caller's (or target's) notifications instead
  --workspace <id|ref|index>  Target workspace (legacy alias: --tab)
  --surface <id|ref|index>    Target surface (legacy alias: --panel); a ref or
                              index resolves inside --workspace, --window or
                              CMUX_WORKSPACE_ID
  --window <id|ref|index>     Target the window's current workspace

Every argument is an option; values may be given as `--opt value` or
`--opt=value`. `--` may only be the last argument.

Examples:
  cmux notify --title Done --body 'All tests passed'
  cmux notify --title=Build --desktop false
  cmux notify --clear
