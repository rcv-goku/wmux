Usage: cmux list-panels [--workspace <id|ref|index>]

List every surface of a workspace (compatibility alias over pane and surface
data), one per line:
  * surface:4  pane:2  terminal  <title>  [focused]

Options:
  --workspace <id|ref|index>    Workspace (default: $CMUX_WORKSPACE_ID)
  --window <id|ref|index>       Window context for refs and indexes
  --json                        Print the full response
