Usage: cmux split-off --surface <id|ref|index> <left|right|up|down>

Move an existing surface into a new split next to its pane. Focus does not
change unless --focus true is given.

Options:
  --surface <id|ref|index>      Surface to split off (required; alias --panel)
  <left|right|up|down>          Side of the new split (required)
  --workspace <id|ref|index>    Workspace context (default: $CMUX_WORKSPACE_ID)
  --window <id|ref|index>       Window context for refs and indexes
  --focus <true|false>          Focus the surface (default: false)
  --json                        Print the full response
