Usage: cmux new-surface [--pane <id|ref|index>] [--type <terminal|browser>] [--command <text>]

Open a new surface (tab) in a pane.

Options:
  --pane <id|ref|index>         Pane for the new surface (default: the
                                focused pane of the workspace)
  --type <terminal|browser>     Content of the new surface (default: terminal)
  --workspace <id|ref|index>    Workspace (default: $CMUX_WORKSPACE_ID)
  --window <id|ref|index>       Window context for refs and indexes
  --url <url>                   Page for a browser surface
  --command <text>              Type <text> plus Enter into the new shell
                                (terminal surfaces only)
  --focus <true|false>          Focus the new surface (default: false)
  --json                        Print the full response
