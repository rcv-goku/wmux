Usage: cmux join-pane --target-pane <id|ref|index> [--workspace <id|ref|index>] [--pane <id|ref|index>] [--surface <id|ref|index>] [--window <id|ref|index>] [--focus <true|false>] [--no-focus]

Move the selected pane or surface into another pane.

Options:
  --target-pane <id|ref|index>  Destination pane (required)
  --pane <id|ref|index>         Pane to move
  --surface <id|ref|index>      Surface to move (default: $CMUX_SURFACE_ID)
  --workspace <id|ref|index>    Workspace context (default: $CMUX_WORKSPACE_ID)
  --window <id|ref|index>       Window context for refs and indexes
  --focus <true|false>          Focus the moved surface (default: false);
                                --no-focus is the same as --focus false
  --json                        Print the full response
