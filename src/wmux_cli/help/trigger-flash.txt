Usage: cmux trigger-flash [--workspace <id|ref|index>] [--surface <id|ref|index>] [--window <id|ref|index>]

Flash a workspace's or surface's pane outline. Defaults to the calling pane
(CMUX_WORKSPACE_ID / CMUX_SURFACE_ID; inside tmux the surface variable is
ignored). --panel is an alias for --surface. Prints `OK`.
