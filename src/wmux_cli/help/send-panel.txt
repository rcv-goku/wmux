Usage: cmux send-panel [--panel <id|ref|index>] [--force] <text>

Type text into a terminal panel (legacy form of send with a required
--panel). Same escapes, draft guard and --force as send.

Options:
  --panel <id|ref|index>        Target surface (required)
  --workspace <id|ref|index>    Workspace context (default: $CMUX_WORKSPACE_ID)
  --window <id|ref|index>       Window context for refs and indexes
  --force                       Skip the draft guard
  --json                        Print the full response

Example:
  cmux send-panel --panel surface:2 "echo hello\n"
