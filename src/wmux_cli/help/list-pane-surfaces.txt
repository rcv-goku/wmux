Usage: cmux list-pane-surfaces [--pane <id|ref|index>]

List the surfaces of a pane, one per line:
  * surface:4  <title>  [selected]

Options:
  --pane <id|ref|index>         Pane (default: the focused pane)
  --workspace <id|ref|index>    Workspace (default: $CMUX_WORKSPACE_ID)
  --window <id|ref|index>       Window context for refs and indexes
  --json                        Print the full response
