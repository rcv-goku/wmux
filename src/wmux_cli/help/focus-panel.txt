Usage: cmux focus-panel --panel <id|ref|index>

Select and focus a surface by its panel handle (the older spelling of
surface focus; the handle may also be given positionally).

Options:
  --panel <id|ref|index>        Surface to focus (required; aliases --surface,
                                --tab)
  --workspace <id|ref|index>    Workspace context (default: $CMUX_WORKSPACE_ID)
  --window <id|ref|index>       Window context for refs and indexes
  --json                        Print the full response
