Usage: cmux rename-tab [--surface <id|ref|index>] <title>

Rename a tab (compatibility wrapper for `tab-action rename`).

Options:
  <title>                       New title (or --title <text>)
  --surface <id|ref|index>      Tab to rename (alias --tab; default:
                                $CMUX_SURFACE_ID)
  --workspace <id|ref|index>    Workspace context (default: $CMUX_WORKSPACE_ID)
  --window <id|ref|index>       Window context for refs and indexes
  --json                        Print the full response
