Usage: cmux drag-surface-to-split --surface <id|ref|index> <left|right|up|down>

Give a surface a split of its own on one side, exactly as dropping its tab
on that edge of a pane does.

Options:
  --surface <id|ref|index>      Surface to move (required; alias --panel)
  <left|right|up|down>          Pane edge (required)
  --workspace <id|ref|index>    Workspace context (default: $CMUX_WORKSPACE_ID)
  --window <id|ref|index>       Window context for refs and indexes
  --focus <true|false>          Focus the surface (default: false)
  --json                        Print the full response
