# W0-A2: Window extraction and model

- No more workspace and tab limits: a window used to stop at 16
  workspaces and a pane at 64 tabs. Session restore now brings back every
  saved workspace and tab (it dropped workspaces past 16 and tabs past
  64).
- Closing a tab in a split pane that is not the focused one (its shell
  exited, or `wmux +tab close --pane N`) no longer switches the tab of
  the pane you are working in or moves keyboard focus there.
- Closing the last tab of the focused split pane no longer leaves wmux
  pointing at the freed pane (which could crash or misbehave on the next
  tab operation); the first remaining pane becomes the focused one.
- Dragging a divider of a nested split follows the cursor (it jumped,
  because the drag was measured against the whole window), and
  `resize_split` moves the divider by the requested number of pixels of
  its own split.
- Dragging a tab inside a split pane's own tab bar reorders the right
  tabs, and a click on a tab bar before its first paint hits the tab
  under the cursor.
- A new tab no longer inherits the attention ring or the
  synchronized-input flag of the tab that used to sit at its position.
- The backend picker ("+" dropdown, "Split ... With...") and the "Set
  default shell..." picker list every installed WSL distribution (they
  stopped at 10 and 30).
- Context menu commands act on what was right-clicked even when tabs or
  workspaces change while the menu is open, and do nothing if it is gone.
- Tab titles longer than 255 bytes are cut at a character boundary
  instead of falling back to "Ghostty".
- Breaking a tab out of its pane no longer leaks memory, and a failed
  break puts the tab back.
